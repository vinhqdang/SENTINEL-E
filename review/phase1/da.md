## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether each headline claim traces down an unbroken inferential chain to specific reported evidence under the conditions actually tested; whether the strongest available counter-explanation of the results is named and excluded rather than ignored; whether formal results and empirical results are claimed to support each other only to the extent their stated assumptions and scope permit; whether scope language ("in general", "in practice", "for deployment") stays inside the evidential envelope; whether disclosed limitations and negative results are carried through into the conclusions and abstract rather than quarantined in a limitations section; whether the selection of reported comparisons, baselines, settings, and metrics is principled and pre-committed rather than assembled after the fact in the favourable direction; whether stakeholders materially affected by the claimed application are represented in how success is defined; and whether the contribution, granted in full, licenses anything a reader did not already possess.
what_triggers_block: A load-bearing claim in the abstract, contributions list, or conclusion is not entailed by the evidence offered for it — the chain has a missing or reversed link, generalises beyond the conditions actually evaluated, treats a formal result as though its assumptions were empirically established, or rests on a comparison selected in a way that would not have been chosen ex ante — such that the claim as written must be materially narrowed or re-evidenced before the paper is sound, and the defect is repairable by revision because the underlying work supports some weaker but real version of the claim.
what_triggers_warn: The central argument reaches its conclusion, but with an identifiable soft joint that does not by itself unseat the thesis: a plausible alternative explanation is left unaddressed though the evidence still favours the paper's reading; a claim is stated more broadly than the evidence warrants in secondary passages while the substantiated version is available elsewhere in the text; a disclosed limitation is acknowledged but its consequences are not propagated into the framing; a relevant stakeholder perspective is absent from how outcomes are judged; or the "so what?" is real but under-argued — in each case the fix is qualification, argument, or acknowledgement rather than retraction or new evidence.
what_triggers_fatal: The core thesis cannot be salvaged by any revision short of new work, because the argument is defective at its foundation — the conclusion is circular or assumes what it sets out to establish; the evidence presented, read correctly, is consistent with or supports the negation of the central claim; the paper's own disclosed negative results or admitted conditions falsify the thesis it nonetheless asserts; the claimed contribution is internally contradictory across the manuscript such that no single coherent thesis exists to evaluate; or, granting every claim in full, nothing whatsoever follows that a reader did not already hold, leaving no defensible contribution to narrow to.
