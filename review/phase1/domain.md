## Scoring Plan

### D2: domain_accuracy

dimension_id: D2
what_to_look_for: Every verifiable domain assertion — dataset and benchmark facts (e.g. Mivia-IWDD, the 2026 IWDD contest field, TACO, ZeroWaste, UAVVaste, AerialWaste, MARIDA, MADOS: modality, platform, scale, annotation granularity, class taxonomy, official splits and prescribed metrics), definitions of detection/segmentation/monitoring tasks and of the metrics reported, attributions of specific results or numbers to specific cited papers, statements of the form "no prior work does/reports X", the literature gap the contribution is positioned against, and the empirical premises about illegal-dumping surveillance conditions (imbalance, scene variability, deployment/streaming regime) — each checked against what the cited source and the field's current evidence actually say, with the check recorded per claim as verified, unverifiable from the manuscript, or contradicted.
what_triggers_block: One or more load-bearing domain errors that a correction would materially change — a stated dataset, benchmark-protocol or metric fact that is wrong (not merely imprecise); a characterisation of a cited work's method, scope, results, or of what it "does not report", that is contradicted by that work rather than being one defensible reading among several; a numeric result or capability attributed to a source that the source does not contain; or a claimed literature gap that is occupied by work the manuscript either omits or dismisses on grounds its own cited evidence does not support, such that the novelty framing depends on the misstatement.
what_triggers_warn: Domain statements that are defensible but under-specified or dated rather than false — a benchmark referenced without the protocol variant, split or metric definition needed to interpret the comparison; terminology used loosely or non-standardly where the intended meaning is still recoverable from context; a gap claim that is narrowly true as written but rests on unstated qualifiers, or omits closely adjacent work that a domain reader would expect cited without that omission overturning the claim; an empirical premise about the surveillance setting asserted without citation where citable evidence exists; or an over-broad generalisation from one dataset or modality to the domain at large.
what_triggers_fatal: A pattern of misrepresentation rather than an isolated error — the manuscript's central positioning rests on a domain claim that is systematically wrong and cannot be repaired by correction because correcting it removes the stated contribution, e.g. a gap claim directly refuted by prior work the manuscript itself cites, multiple cited works recast to state the opposite of what they report, or fabricated/non-existent datasets, benchmarks, results or citations presented as established domain fact.
