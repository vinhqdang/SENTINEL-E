## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, owned by the methodology seat).** Whether the study's design, data handling, statistical reporting, and reproduction affordances would survive a competent referee in statistical machine learning. Not my seat to adjudicate; editorially it is mandatory, so a fatal finding ends the submission under F1 and a block forces major revision under F2. I defer wholly to Reviewer 1.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain seat).** Whether claims are consonant with current field evidence, prior work is characterised faithfully rather than conveniently, and domain terminology and reported results are factually correct. My interest is reputational: misrepresented prior art in a collection paper propagates into a specialist literature that reads the collection as curated. Not my seat.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by the Devil's Advocate, methodology also eligible).** Whether the central thesis holds together on its own terms — whether evidence adduced bears on the claims made. Distinct from rigour: a paper can be methodologically clean and still argue for a conclusion its results do not reach. Dual-eligible, so DA/methodology disagreement is a signal to record rather than smooth over.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high, owned by the perspective seat).** Whether framing, definitions and implications are intelligible to adjacent-field readers and whether cross-boundary claims are substantiated. For this collection the readership spans applied ML practitioners, environmental-monitoring specialists and municipal-systems researchers without a common vocabulary. High rather than mandatory: a block yields major revision under F4 without being terminal.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal, my seat).** Whether the manuscript is organised so a reader can find the contribution; exposition clear at sentence and section level; figures and tables self-supporting; venue conventions observed for length, structure, notation and supporting material. Normal priority means deficiencies produce minor revision under F5 — but "normal priority" is not "cosmetic": presentation failures that make the contribution unassessable are substantive, and I will treat them as such while keeping them inside this dimension rather than laundering them into D6.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, my seat).** Whether the manuscript belongs in this journal and collection, and whether it makes a contribution genuinely original, genuinely significant, and appropriately pitched. Fit is not keyword overlap; originality is not novelty of application alone; significance is not competence. Mandatory, so my finding can independently reject (F1) or force major revision (F2); I therefore commit in advance to a demanding, specific standard rather than a discretionary impression formed after the fact.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Whether an informed reader can locate and assess the contribution on a single careful pass — a title, abstract and introduction that state the actual claim rather than the topic; an explicit statement of what is contributed and what is not; section ordering that lets formal material, protocol and results be read without forward references; notation introduced before use and used consistently; every figure and table self-supporting via caption, axis labels, units, sample sizes and defined symbols; explicit signposting of which supporting artefacts back which reported number; and conformance to the venue's conventions on length (against the declared 9,700 words), structure, reference formatting, and the placement of proofs and supplementary material.
what_triggers_block: Any reported quantitative result, table or figure that cannot be tied to a stated experimental condition and evaluation protocol from the text alone; or a central formal or technical object used substantively before it is defined anywhere; or a figure/table carrying a headline claim that lacks the labelling, units or scale needed to read it; or an abstract or introduction whose stated claim does not match the claim the results section actually supports; or organisation so disordered that assessing the contribution requires reconstructing the argument out of order across non-adjacent sections.
what_triggers_warn: Localised but repeated presentation defects that slow assessment without preventing it — inconsistent notation or terminology across sections, several figures needing caption or labelling work, uneven section proportions, avoidable repetition between sections, reference-list or citation-format irregularities, or overrun/underrun against venue length and structure conventions requiring a targeted rewrite rather than a restructure.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Whether the contribution is one this journal's applied-machine-learning readership can use and this collection's smart-waste-monitoring and environmental-surveillance audience specifically needs — a clearly delimited statement of what is new relative to named, current alternatives rather than a generic strawman; explicit positioning against the closest competing methods with the delta stated in terms a practitioner could act on; a defensible account of why the environmental-surveillance setting is essential rather than a wrapper on a method demonstrable anywhere; significance argued through consequences for deployment, decision-making or subsequent research rather than asserted; honest scoping of the empirical basis including whether evidence is simulated and what that licenses; and a match between the venue's applied orientation and the paper's centre of gravity.
what_triggers_block: The actual contribution is generic to its methodological subfield with the surveillance setting supplying only motivation, framing or nomenclature, so that removing the application domain would leave the contribution intact; or the claimed novelty is not distinguished from at least one identifiable prior approach a reader of this collection would immediately have in mind; or significance for the readership is asserted rather than argued, with no articulated path from result to practice or further work; or the substantive centre sits so far from applied machine learning that its natural home is a pure-theory or pure-domain venue and the fit is nominal.
what_triggers_warn: Fit and contribution are real but under-defended — the delta over the nearest prior work is stated qualitatively where a concrete comparison was available; practical implications are gestured at in a closing paragraph rather than developed; the framing is pitched to one constituency at the expense of the other; limitations of the evidence base are acknowledged but not carried through into correspondingly scoped claims; or the originality is genuine but incremental in a way the paper oversells rather than owns.
what_triggers_fatal: The contribution is not a contribution to this venue's literature at all — a restatement, translation or minor reparameterisation of an existing published result presented as new; or the claimed novelty is contradicted by work the manuscript itself cites; or the fit rests on domain vocabulary attached to a contribution with no bearing on environmental surveillance; or the significance claim depends on an application scenario the manuscript concedes elsewhere it cannot support.
