\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Streaming visual-anomaly detectors, including those for illegal dumping, are evaluated per clip but deployed on continuous streams, where alerting at the first threshold crossing is optional stopping. Fed valid 1\% p-values, a per-frame rule raises a false alarm on \ValidityPfaPvaluePct\% of $40$-minute null streams. Theoretically, we introduce the episodic e-process, a mixture over episodes that start, flicker, end and recur, and prove that it is a non-negative supermartingale, so Ville's inequality gives time-uniform false-alarm control; it contains the changepoint mixture as the case where episodes never end. Algorithmically, a two-state forward recursion evaluates it in constant time per frame, fed by conformal p-values with exact calibration-conditional Beta levels and combined across cameras by e-BH. Empirically, SENTINEL-E holds its level over simulated week-long streams on which per-frame thresholds fail within an hour, and paired comparisons show that episodes shorten detection delay for long, intermittent, recurring events but not for single or permanent changes. On real video (UCSD Ped2, CUHK Avenue and Mivia-IWDD, each with optical-flow and PatchCore backbones), operating points are selected on training videos only and each test video is monitored alone. Per-video false-alarm rates stay within sampling error of the nominal level on the single-camera corpora but exceed it in point estimate on Mivia-IWDD at $\alpha=0.1$ (9 of 50 normal videos; 95\% interval 0.09--0.31), where calibration pools many different scenes. Detection power depends on the backbone and remains limited, reaching 10 of 50 dumping events on Mivia-IWDD. The guarantee layer adds under a microsecond per frame.
\end{abstract}

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} video anomaly detection; illegal dumping; anytime-valid inference; e-values; conformal prediction; sequential change detection; video surveillance.

\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
  \item An episodic e-process with a supermartingale guarantee for recurring events
  \item Time-uniform false-alarm control at \CostSentinelUs\,\textmu s per frame
  \item Per-frame thresholds fail within an hour; SENTINEL-E holds over simulated weeks
  \item Real video: per-video false alarms within sampling error, power backbone-limited
  \item Pooled calibration on Mivia-IWDD gives 9 of 50 false alarms at $\alpha=0.1$
\end{itemize}
